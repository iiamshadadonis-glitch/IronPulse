## Archive checks

The complete project is packaged as one standard Deflate ZIP with a single `DropBeat-V1.59` root. It is neither encrypted nor split and does not require Zip64. Paths are checked for traversal, Windows-unsafe names and case collisions.

The final archive is CRC-tested with Python and Info-ZIP, independently extracted by both, and every extracted file is compared with its SHA-256 source hash. `V1.59-MANIFEST.sha256` is checked after extraction. The full current code/database command is rerun from the extracted project. A separately retrieved saved copy is compared byte for byte with the final ZIP, then its CRCs and all entry hashes are checked again.

No production deployment, live payment/refund, external OAuth exchange or real-user push was performed. These checks verify the local implemented behavior and archive integrity; they do not claim live production certification.
