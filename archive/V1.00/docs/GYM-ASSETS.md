Two original photographs were created with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized/encoded for this project. They are placeholders rather than images of the gym's actual staff or premises. Replace them through Admin when real gym photos are available.

Hero prompt: a wide, premium editorial photograph of an adult Indian woman doing a dumbbell exercise with an adult Indian fitness coach in a modern strength gym; people on the right, dark uncluttered space on the left for HTML text; charcoal equipment, subtle indigo accents and natural warm light; realistic anatomy, no logos, text, watermarks or dance imagery.

Interior prompt: a wide architectural photograph of a spacious modern functional-training gym with dumbbells, a rowing machine, stationary bicycle, squat rack and kettlebells; black rubber flooring, charcoal steel, subtle indigo details and natural morning sunlight; no people, logos, signage, text or dance floor.
