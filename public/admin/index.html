Product Image URL

Paste a public HTTPS image, CDN link, or a Google Drive image shared publicly. This source is for the website product gallery. After Save Image, one dual website crop editor opens. You control the 900 × 1100 Product Card crop and the independent 1280 × 720 (16:9) Offer Details crop side by side. The two saved crops are separate and are used only in their matching website locations. The original source image bytes are not stored in Cloudflare D1; only its public URL/metadata and the separate website crop URLs are saved.
