# Final V16.29 – Separate Story Image URL + Offer Details polish

## Product Images / Social Status
- Product Image URL remains the website image source.
- When **Post as social status** is enabled, a new **Story / Status Image URL** field appears.
- Recommended social image size: **1080 × 1920 px (9:16)** for Facebook and Instagram Stories.
- Facebook and Instagram use the supplied Story artwork without adding the old generated offer template.
- Threads, LinkedIn and Pinterest receive safe contain/pad variants of the same social artwork when their API supports image publishing.
- Website crop remains separate and does not repost the social status.

## Offer Details
- Uses a larger 16:9 detail image prepared from the original product image source without stretching.
- About the Plan / You Should Know headings are reduced to a cleaner content-heading size.
- Payment type and trial duration text are removed from the public detail card.
- Trial pricing now makes the amount collected today the strongest price, while previous/catalog prices are struck through.
- Book Now and View More Plans remain.

## Availability / demand messages
- No randomized or visitor-manipulated scarcity is generated.
- Optional **Spot Limit** can be configured on a plan when there is a real capacity.
- Remaining spots are calculated from actual paid order quantities.
- Public cards/detail pages can show truthful states such as Available, Trial offer available, Popular now, Filling fast, Only N spots left, or Currently full depending on real plan data.
