The shared mobile refresh SVG uses Lucide's rotate-cw paths.
Source: https://lucide.dev/icons/rotate-cw
Distribution: https://app.unpkg.com/lucide-react@0.575.0/files/dist/esm/icons/rotate-cw.js
The original 24 x 24 viewBox is retained; the stroke uses the app's 1.8 setting.
No external icon runtime or network request is required.
