# Gym assets

Gym artwork uses the existing theme tokens #4B55BE, #A469A8, #22244f and white.

Included locally in public/assets/:

- ironpulse-logo.svg, ironpulse-logo-white.svg and PNG versions.
- ironpulse-invoice-logo.jpg.
- 192px and 512px standard/maskable PWA icons.
- apple-touch-icon.png at 180px, plus favicons.
- gym-hero.jpg, gym-training.jpg, gym-interior.jpg and gym-avatar.svg.

The photographs are generated fitness placeholders, rather than photographs of your actual gym, staff or members. Replace them through Admin when your real photographs are available.

Compatibility artwork filenames now also use gym naming. Redirects keep earlier asset URLs working. The application does not depend on a remote image service for these placeholders.
